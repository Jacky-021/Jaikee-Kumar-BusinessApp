Book rail freight. Know where every wagon is.

RakeLink helps factories, traders and exporters book wagons, rakes and containers on Indian Railways, then follow each consignment from the loading siding to the destination terminal.

Based in Vadodara, Gujarat. Serving Gujarat, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh and the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor.

One booking desk for the whole rail journey

Most small and mid-size shippers have no railway siding of their own and no team that knows freight tariffs. RakeLink is that team.

What we do

We register wagon demand with Indian Railways, handle the railway receipt and freight payment, arrange trucks to and from the siding, and share live status until the cargo is delivered.

Who we serve

Steel, cement and fertiliser makers, agri-commodity traders, exporters moving containers to ports, and small shippers who book less than a full wagon.

Where we operate

Gujarat, Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh, long-haul lanes from Jamshedpur and Korba into Gujarat, and port lanes to Mundra and JN Port.

Why customers choose us

One booking, one tracking page and one invoice for the rail and road legs together. Customers spend less time chasing wagons and more time running the plant.

Rail references we rely on

  • Freight Operations Information System (FOIS): register wagon demand and check official consignment status.
  • Freight Business Development portal: look up routes and distances before quoting a customer.
  • Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation (DFCCIL): check corridor details for our JN Port to Dadri container lane.
  • Indian Railways: read freight circulars and tariff updates that change our rates.
